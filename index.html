FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

4K Quality Convert FAQ

What does 4K image quality mean?

For many displays, 4K UHD is 3840 × 2160 pixels. Images have different aspect ratios, so this tool sets the long edge to up to 3840 pixels and keeps the original proportions.

Can this tool improve blurry images?

It can resize an image, but resizing alone cannot reliably recover detail missing from a blurry or low-resolution original. AI enhancement is not included.

Which formats can I convert?

This tool accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP images. You can export to WebP, JPG, or PNG.

Are my photos uploaded or stored?

No. This version uses browser-based image processing. The selected image is read locally, and the output is generated in your browser.

Is the converter free?

Yes. This browser-based version does not require an account or payment.
